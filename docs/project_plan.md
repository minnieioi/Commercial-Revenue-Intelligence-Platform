# Project Plan

## Business Goal
Build an end-to-end commercial revenue intelligence platform for a fictional global AdTech / SaaS company.

The platform should help leaders answer:
1. Where is revenue coming from?
2. Which accounts and segments are accelerating or declining?
3. Where is pipeline conversion breaking?
4. Which accounts deserve commercial attention first?
5. What should the business do next?

## Phase 1 — SQL & Data Modeling
- Data quality checks
- Monthly revenue trend
- Revenue by region / industry / tier
- Top-account concentration
- Month-over-month and year-over-year growth
- Pipeline stage analysis
- Aging opportunities
- Closed-won rate by segment

## Phase 2 — Python Analytics
- Pareto analysis
- Growth segmentation
- Account volatility
- Segment mix
- Commercial priority scoring

## Phase 3 — BI Dashboard
Suggested pages:
1. Executive Overview
2. Account & Segment Growth
3. Pipeline & Commercial Actions

## Phase 4 — Executive Recommendations
Translate findings into:
- growth opportunities
- revenue risks
- account priorities
- pipeline actions

## Success Criteria
A recruiter should understand the business problem, data model, analysis, and recommendation within 30 seconds of opening the README.
